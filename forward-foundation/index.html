Mission

Built for stability, health, self-sufficiency, and opportunity.

Forward Foundation Inc. is organized exclusively for charitable and educational purposes. We keep our work broad on purpose: meet people where they are, remove practical barriers, and strengthen the systems that help families and neighborhoods move forward.

California Nonprofit Public Benefit Corporation

Formed to support underserved communities with education, direct assistance, and access to resources under Internal Revenue Code section 501(c)(3) purposes.

Focus

Three ways we show up.

Our work stays flexible so it can meet real local need, while staying anchored to the same three commitments.

  1. 01

    Education

    Learning pathways, skills, and knowledge that expand what comes next for students, caregivers, and adults rebuilding momentum.

  2. 02

    Direct assistance

    Practical support that reduces immediate pressure so people can protect health, housing stability, and day-to-day dignity.

  3. 03

    Access to resources

    Clearer routes to the tools, networks, and opportunities that make self-sufficiency durable instead of temporary.

Approach

Broad enough to adapt. Grounded enough to matter.

We partner with communities, educators, and local organizations to design support that is useful on the ground. The aim is simple: strengthen what already works, fill clear gaps, and leave people with more agency than they started with.

  • Community-led priorities

    Programs follow local need, not a one-size template.

  • Practical first steps

    Assistance is designed to be usable immediately and build toward longer-term stability.

  • Open partnership

    We work alongside schools, neighbors, and organizations already trusted in their communities.

About

A public benefit organization with a clear charge.

Forward Foundation exists to advance charitable and educational work that widens opportunity. Our property and resources are dedicated to that purpose, and our leadership is charged with stewarding them carefully.
